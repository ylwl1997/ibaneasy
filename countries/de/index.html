Banking System in Germany

Germany has one of the largest banking sectors in Europe, with over 1,600 banks including Deutsche Bank, Commerzbank, and hundreds of cooperative and savings banks (Sparkassen, Volksbanken). All German IBANs start with DE followed by 20 characters including the 8-digit BLZ (Bankleitzahl) bank code. IBAN has been mandatory for all domestic and cross-border SEPA transfers since 2014.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Germany?

Germany uses a 22-character IBAN with the country code "DE" followed by two check digits and a 18-character BBAN. The format is 8!n10!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 12.

Is Germany a SEPA country?

Yes, Germany is part of the SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) and uses IBAN for all domestic and international transfers.

How long is a Germany IBAN?

A Germany IBAN is exactly 22 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 18 characters.

How do I find my Germany IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
