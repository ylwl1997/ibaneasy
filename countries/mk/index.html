Banking System in North Macedonia

North Macedonia uses the IBAN system for international bank transfers. The North Macedonia IBAN is 19 characters long and consists of the ISO country code "MK", two MOD-97 check digits, and a Basic Bank Account Number (BBAN) containing the domestic bank code (3 digits), account number, and any branch/routing identifiers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for North Macedonia?

North Macedonia uses a 19-character IBAN with the country code "MK" followed by two check digits and a 15-character BBAN. The format is 3!n10!c2!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 7.

Is North Macedonia a SEPA country?

No, North Macedonia is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a North Macedonia IBAN?

A North Macedonia IBAN is exactly 19 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 15 characters.

How do I find my North Macedonia IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
