Banking System in Austria

Austria has a well-established banking system with Erste Group, Raiffeisen Bank International, and Bank Austria. Austrian IBANs are 20 characters and include a 5-digit bank code (BLZ) and an 11-digit account number. IBAN is mandatory for all SEPA transfers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Austria?

Austria uses a 20-character IBAN with the country code "AT" followed by two check digits and a 16-character BBAN. The format is 5!n11!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 9.

Is Austria a SEPA country?

Yes, Austria is part of the SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) and uses IBAN for all domestic and international transfers.

How long is a Austria IBAN?

A Austria IBAN is exactly 20 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 16 characters.

How do I find my Austria IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
