Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for French Polynesia?

French Polynesia uses a 27-character IBAN with the country code "PF" followed by two check digits and a 23-character BBAN. The format is 5!n5!n11!c2!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 9.

Is French Polynesia a SEPA country?

Yes, French Polynesia is part of the SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) and uses IBAN for all domestic and international transfers.

How long is a French Polynesia IBAN?

A French Polynesia IBAN is exactly 27 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 23 characters.

How do I find my French Polynesia IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
