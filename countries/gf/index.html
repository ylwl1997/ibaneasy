Banking System in French Guiana

French Guiana is a SEPA member country. All SEPA credit transfers and direct debits in French Guiana require a valid IBAN. The French Guiana IBAN is 27 characters long and consists of the ISO country code "GF", two MOD-97 check digits, and a Basic Bank Account Number (BBAN) containing the domestic bank code (5 digits), account number, and any branch/routing identifiers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for French Guiana?

French Guiana uses a 27-character IBAN with the country code "GF" followed by two check digits and a 23-character BBAN. The format is 5!n5!n11!c2!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 9.

Is French Guiana a SEPA country?

Yes, French Guiana is part of the SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) and uses IBAN for all domestic and international transfers.

How long is a French Guiana IBAN?

A French Guiana IBAN is exactly 27 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 23 characters.

How do I find my French Guiana IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
