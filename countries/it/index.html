Banking System in Italy

Italy has over 400 banks, led by UniCredit, Intesa Sanpaolo, and Banco BPM. Italian IBANs are 27 characters with a 5-digit bank code (ABI), 5-digit branch code (CAB), and 12-digit account number. The CIN (Control Internal Number) check character precedes the BBAN.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Italy?

Italy uses a 27-character IBAN with the country code "IT" followed by two check digits and a 23-character BBAN. The format is 1!a5!n5!n12!c. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 9.

Is Italy a SEPA country?

Yes, Italy is part of the SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) and uses IBAN for all domestic and international transfers.

How long is a Italy IBAN?

A Italy IBAN is exactly 27 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 23 characters.

How do I find my Italy IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
