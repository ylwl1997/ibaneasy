Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Saudi Arabia?

Saudi Arabia uses a 24-character IBAN with the country code "SA" followed by two check digits and a 20-character BBAN. The format is 2!n18!c. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 6.

Is Saudi Arabia a SEPA country?

No, Saudi Arabia is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a Saudi Arabia IBAN?

A Saudi Arabia IBAN is exactly 24 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 20 characters.

How do I find my Saudi Arabia IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
