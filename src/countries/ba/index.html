Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Bosnia and Herzegovina?

Bosnia and Herzegovina uses a 20-character IBAN with the country code "BA" followed by two check digits and a 16-character BBAN. The format is 3!n3!n8!n2!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 7.

Is Bosnia and Herzegovina a SEPA country?

No, Bosnia and Herzegovina is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a Bosnia and Herzegovina IBAN?

A Bosnia and Herzegovina IBAN is exactly 20 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 16 characters.

How do I find my Bosnia and Herzegovina IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
