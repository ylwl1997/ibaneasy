Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Costa Rica?

Costa Rica uses a 22-character IBAN with the country code "CR" followed by two check digits and a 18-character BBAN. The format is 4!n14!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 8.

Is Costa Rica a SEPA country?

No, Costa Rica is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a Costa Rica IBAN?

A Costa Rica IBAN is exactly 22 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 18 characters.

How do I find my Costa Rica IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
