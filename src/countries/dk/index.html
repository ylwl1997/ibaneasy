Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Denmark?

Denmark uses a 18-character IBAN with the country code "DK" followed by two check digits and a 14-character BBAN. The format is 4!n10!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 8.

Is Denmark a SEPA country?

Yes, Denmark is part of the SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) and uses IBAN for all domestic and international transfers.

How long is a Denmark IBAN?

A Denmark IBAN is exactly 18 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 14 characters.

How do I find my Denmark IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
