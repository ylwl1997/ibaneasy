Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Wallis and Futuna?

Wallis and Futuna uses a 27-character IBAN with the country code "WF" followed by two check digits and a 23-character BBAN. The format is 5!n5!n11!c2!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 9.

Is Wallis and Futuna a SEPA country?

Yes, Wallis and Futuna is part of the SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) and uses IBAN for all domestic and international transfers.

How long is a Wallis and Futuna IBAN?

A Wallis and Futuna IBAN is exactly 27 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 23 characters.

How do I find my Wallis and Futuna IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
