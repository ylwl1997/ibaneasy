Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Qatar?

Qatar uses a 29-character IBAN with the country code "QA" followed by two check digits and a 25-character BBAN. The format is 4!a21!c. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 8.

Is Qatar a SEPA country?

No, Qatar is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a Qatar IBAN?

A Qatar IBAN is exactly 29 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 25 characters.

How do I find my Qatar IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
