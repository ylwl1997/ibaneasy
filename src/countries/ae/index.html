Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for United Arab Emirates?

United Arab Emirates uses a 23-character IBAN with the country code "AE" followed by two check digits and a 19-character BBAN. The format is 3!n16!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 7.

Is United Arab Emirates a SEPA country?

No, United Arab Emirates is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a United Arab Emirates IBAN?

A United Arab Emirates IBAN is exactly 23 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 19 characters.

How do I find my United Arab Emirates IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
