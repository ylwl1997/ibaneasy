Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Timor-Leste?

Timor-Leste uses a 23-character IBAN with the country code "TL" followed by two check digits and a 19-character BBAN. The format is 4!n15!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 8.

Is Timor-Leste a SEPA country?

No, Timor-Leste is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a Timor-Leste IBAN?

A Timor-Leste IBAN is exactly 23 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 19 characters.

How do I find my Timor-Leste IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
