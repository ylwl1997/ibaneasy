Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for São Tomé and Príncipe?

São Tomé and Príncipe uses a 25-character IBAN with the country code "ST" followed by two check digits and a 21-character BBAN. The format is 8!n13!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 12.

Is São Tomé and Príncipe a SEPA country?

No, São Tomé and Príncipe is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a São Tomé and Príncipe IBAN?

A São Tomé and Príncipe IBAN is exactly 25 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 21 characters.

How do I find my São Tomé and Príncipe IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
