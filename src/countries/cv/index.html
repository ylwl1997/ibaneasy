Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Cape Verde?

Cape Verde uses a 25-character IBAN with the country code "CV" followed by two check digits and a 21-character BBAN. The format is 4!n17!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 8.

Is Cape Verde a SEPA country?

No, Cape Verde is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a Cape Verde IBAN?

A Cape Verde IBAN is exactly 25 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 21 characters.

How do I find my Cape Verde IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
