Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Armenia?

Armenia uses a 28-character IBAN with the country code "AM" followed by two check digits and a 24-character BBAN. The format is 5!n20!c. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 9.

Is Armenia a SEPA country?

No, Armenia is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a Armenia IBAN?

A Armenia IBAN is exactly 28 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 24 characters.

How do I find my Armenia IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
