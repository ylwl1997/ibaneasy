Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for British Virgin Islands?

British Virgin Islands uses a 24-character IBAN with the country code "VG" followed by two check digits and a 20-character BBAN. The format is 4!a16!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 8.

Is British Virgin Islands a SEPA country?

No, British Virgin Islands is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a British Virgin Islands IBAN?

A British Virgin Islands IBAN is exactly 24 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 20 characters.

How do I find my British Virgin Islands IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
