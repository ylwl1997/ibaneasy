Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Saint Lucia?

Saint Lucia uses a 32-character IBAN with the country code "LC" followed by two check digits and a 28-character BBAN. The format is 4!a24!c. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 8.

Is Saint Lucia a SEPA country?

No, Saint Lucia is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a Saint Lucia IBAN?

A Saint Lucia IBAN is exactly 32 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 28 characters.

How do I find my Saint Lucia IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
