Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Montenegro?

Montenegro uses a 22-character IBAN with the country code "ME" followed by two check digits and a 18-character BBAN. The format is 3!n13!n2!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 7.

Is Montenegro a SEPA country?

No, Montenegro is not a SEPA member, but uses IBAN for international transfers.

How long is a Montenegro IBAN?

A Montenegro IBAN is exactly 22 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 18 characters.

How do I find my Montenegro IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
