Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Vatican City?

Vatican City uses a 22-character IBAN with the country code "VA" followed by two check digits and a 18-character BBAN. The format is 3!n15!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 7.

Is Vatican City a SEPA country?

Yes, Vatican City is part of the SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) and uses IBAN for all domestic and international transfers.

How long is a Vatican City IBAN?

A Vatican City IBAN is exactly 22 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 18 characters.

How do I find my Vatican City IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
