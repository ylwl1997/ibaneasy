Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Jersey?

Jersey uses a 22-character IBAN with the country code "JE" followed by two check digits and a 18-character BBAN. The format is 4!a6!n8!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 8.

Is Jersey a SEPA country?

Yes, Jersey is part of the SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) and uses IBAN for all domestic and international transfers.

How long is a Jersey IBAN?

A Jersey IBAN is exactly 22 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 18 characters.

How do I find my Jersey IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
