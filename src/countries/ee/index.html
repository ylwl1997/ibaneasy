Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBAN format for Estonia?

Estonia uses a 20-character IBAN with the country code "EE" followed by two check digits and a 16-character BBAN. The format is 4!n12!n. The bank code occupies positions 5 to 8.

Is Estonia a SEPA country?

Yes, Estonia is part of the SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) and uses IBAN for all domestic and international transfers.

How long is a Estonia IBAN?

A Estonia IBAN is exactly 20 characters long. The BBAN portion accounts for 16 characters.

How do I find my Estonia IBAN?

Your IBAN is printed on your bank statement, available in your online banking portal, or can be obtained from your bank. It typically appears alongside your BIC/SWIFT code for international transfers.
